The program

What is APIE Camp?

APIE stands for Asia Pacific Internet Engineering. It is a program designed and run by SOI Asia, with support from APNIC, that teaches students across the region how the Internet is actually built and operated.

The camp is the on-site part of the program: five days of project-based work on designing and deploying a campus network, using what students learned in the online courses and the e-Workshop. Participants work in groups and come from universities all over Asia.

Camp 11 is hosted in Kathmandu. It follows Camp 10 in Hanoi, Vietnam in August 2026, and Camp 12 is planned for Bali, Indonesia in January 2027.

Invitation only

The camp is open to learners who have completed APIE Online Courses 1 and 2 and the APIE e-Workshop.

The host

Tribhuvan University

Camp 11 takes place at Tribhuvan University (TU), Nepal’s oldest and largest public university. It is named after King Tribhuvan and its main campus sits in Kirtipur, on the edge of the Kathmandu Valley.

TU is government-financed and spans constituent campuses and affiliated colleges across the country, which makes it a fitting home for a camp about connecting people through networks.

Visit tu.edu.np

APIE is developed and taught by experienced professionals and trainers who share the challenges facing Internet engineers in the region.

Organizer

The School on Internet Asia consortium, founded in 2001 by the WIDE Project, which designs and operates the APIE program with partner universities.
